# Coding in Color: context for copy and demo

Summarized from CIC's one-pager (shared Nov 15). Use for the README, demo script, empty states and funder report wording. Figures are CIC's own; cite them as "CIC reports".

- **Who:** Coding in Color is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit developing Arizona's future digital workforce through digital skilling, computer science and AI education. Vision: every student in Arizona has access to a computer science, AI or technical education. Site: https://codingincolor.net
- **Programs** (seed program list candidates): Workshops and Hackathons; Summer Camps (week-long, a new topic daily); Computer Science Courses (120+ hours, certifications); AI Courses (40+ hours, AI fluency and ethics); CSPD / AIPD (7-week educator cohorts); CS Curriculum Consulting for districts.
- **Partners named:** Phoenix College, University of Advancing Technology, Agua Fria High School District, Maricopa County Regional School District, Salt River Schools.
- **2025 impact (CIC reports):** 550+ students, 2,500+ instruction hours, 80%+ from historically disadvantaged backgrounds. AI Student Academy, AI Teacher Academy and AI Summer Camp report large knowledge and confidence gains; see the one-pager for exact figures before quoting them.

Why it matters for CIC Compass: board members are volunteers who raise money for these programs. Funder exports and thank-you templates should name the program the gift supported and use outcomes like the ones above.
